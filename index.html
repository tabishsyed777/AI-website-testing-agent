A simple loop

From plain language to a reproducible bug.

Keep human judgment in the loop. Pathfinder is envisioned as a teammate that explores and documents, while your team decides what to fix.

Set the goal

Choose a page and describe the outcome you want to test, such as signing up or placing an order.

Let the agent explore

It navigates the flow, checks expected outcomes, and captures what it sees along the way.

Review the evidence

Inspect a concise report with steps, screenshots, and issues ready for a human to confirm.

Make every user journey feel effortless.
